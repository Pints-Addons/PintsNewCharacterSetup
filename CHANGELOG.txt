Pint's New Character Setup - Changelog

v1.0.5
- WoW Forever: when the game hides a cooldown number from add-ons, it gets the smaller size (it was left at full size
  and spilled off the button). /pns cd (hovering a button) shows what its cooldown number is.

v1.0.4
- Cooldown numbers on smaller action bars (e.g. bars 2 and 7) now shrink properly: 3+ characters ("23m") take the smaller
  size, and any number too wide for its button shrinks until it fits.

v1.0.3
- Cooldown numbers back to your full size for 1-2 characters ("9", "45"); 3 or more ("56m", "1:26") a size down so they
  fit on the button.

v1.0.2
- New default "Left-click only targets": left-clicking a mob or corpse targets it, right-click loots and talks
  (WoW Forever looted and talked on left-click). Given once to existing characters too.

v1.0.1
- Cooldown numbers never grow bigger than fits their button (a long cooldown's "1:26" spilled past small
  action buttons).

v1.0
- First public release. Combines four small add-ons (Auto Loot On, Chat Class Colors, Cooldown Style, Stack Size).
- Every new character starts with your chosen defaults: Auto Loot, cooldown numbers, class-coloured chat names,
  Target of Target, maximum camera distance, quest tracking and (optionally) loot window at mouse.
- Options page (/setup) to choose the defaults, plus "Apply to this character now".
- Large, outlined cooldown numbers with adjustable size and colour.
- "Stacks to" and "Used in" lines on item tooltips, each switchable.
